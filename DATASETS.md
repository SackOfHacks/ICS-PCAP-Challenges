# Where the captures are

Every challenge reads its captures from `data/<dataset>/`, which `scripts/fetch.py` fills: 70 datasets, ~351 GB once fetched.

- **41 are mirrored in this repository's [`data-v1` release](https://github.com/SackOfHacks/ICS-PCAP-Challenges/releases/tag/data-v1)**: their licence (or their authors) allow redistribution. `fetch.py` downloads the parts, checks every SHA-256 against `catalog/datasets.json` and unpacks them.
- **29 are downloaded from their original sources**: they were published without a licence that allows re-hosting.

Every challenge page starts with a **Get the captures** section with the exact command and these links. Without `fetch.py`: download every part of a dataset, `cat <dataset>.tar.part* | tar -x`, then `gunzip` the files listed in its `MIRROR-GZIPPED.txt`.

_Generated by `scripts/build.py` from `catalog/datasets.json`. Credits and licences: [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md)._
